Collecting and Using Your Personal Data

Types of Data Collected

Personal Data

While using Our Service, We may ask You to provide Us with certain personally identifiable information:

  • GitHub Username: Used to fetch your public contribution graph.
  • GitHub Personal Access Token: Stored locally on your device using platform-specific encryption (Keystore/Secure Storage). It is never sent to our servers.

Usage Data

Usage Data is collected automatically when using the Service. This may include your Device's IP address, device model, operating system, and anonymous usage statistics for stability improvements.

Data Handling - Specifics for GitWall

GitHub Authentication

Your GitHub Personal Access Token is used only to fetch your contribution data from the GitHub API. It is never sent to any third-party server or collected by us.

Crash Reporting (Firebase Crashlytics)

We use Firebase Crashlytics to collect anonymous crash reports to improve app stability. This is only enabled with your explicit consent, which can be withdrawn at any time in the app settings. All reports are sanitized to remove sensitive data.

Children's Privacy

Our Service does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from anyone under the age of 13.

We may update Our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify You of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
